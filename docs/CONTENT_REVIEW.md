# Bangladesh content review checklist

- Review husbandry recommendations with a Bangladesh poultry specialist.
- Confirm current Department of Livestock Services guidance for vaccination and disease response.
- Avoid unsupported drug doses and blanket antibiotic recommendations.
- Label prices as user-entered estimates, not current market quotations.
- Distinguish broiler, layer, Sonali and indigenous chicken management.
- Add sources and review dates to technical lessons.
